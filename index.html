01 Professional overview

From the ledger to the lakehouse

I started in finance, reconciling bank statements and proving ledgers. I moved into payments, then led financial planning and Board reporting. Each step put me closer to the data behind the numbers, until building that data became the job.

That path shapes how I work. I agree definitions before I build, I check totals before and after every join, and I explain results in the language finance and leadership use.

Alongside my main role, I have worked part-time as a BI analyst for a pan-African automotive finance company.

03 Approach

How I work

The same six steps on every project, from the first conversation to production. Nothing reaches a report until it passes the checks on the right.

  1. 01

    Agree the definitions

    What counts as a sale, which amount is revenue, which date sets the week.

  2. 02

    Land the raw data

    Source data kept as received, so every figure can be traced back.

  3. 03

    Apply the rules

    Types, dates, channel mapping, duplicates and eligibility in one place.

  4. 04

    Build at a declared grain

    One row per entity before any join, so totals cannot multiply.

  5. 05

    Reconcile

    Counts and amounts checked against the source and finance before use.

  6. 06

    Deploy and explain

    Into production, with results explained in the language finance uses.
